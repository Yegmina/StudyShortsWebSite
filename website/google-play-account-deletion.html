Account Deletion

StudyShorts Account and Data Deletion Request

Effective date: March 20, 2026

This page explains how users of the StudyShorts app can request deletion of their account and related personal data. This information is provided for Google Play account deletion requirements.

1. HOW TO REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION

To request deletion of your StudyShorts account and related data, please email us at main@studyshorts.net.

Please include the following in your message:

  1. The email address connected to your StudyShorts account
  2. A clear statement that you want your account deleted
  3. Any details that help us identify your account and process the request correctly

For faster processing, you can use the subject line Account Deletion Request - StudyShorts.

2. WHAT WILL BE DELETED

When your deletion request is processed, we will delete or anonymize, as applicable:

  • Your StudyShorts account profile information
  • Your login-related account records
  • Your uploaded study materials and generated learning content associated with your account
  • Your in-app progress and learning activity connected to your account
  • Support communications associated only with the deleted account, where deletion is permitted

3. WHAT MAY BE RETAINED

Some limited information may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

  • Technical logs and security records
  • Records needed to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes
  • Backup copies that are automatically removed in the normal retention cycle

4. RETENTION PERIOD

We aim to process account deletion requests within a reasonable time after verifying the request. In some cases, certain retained data may be kept for up to 30 days after deletion processing, or longer if required by law or necessary for legitimate security and abuse-prevention purposes.
